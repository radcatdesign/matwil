Questions

How is Operating Partner different from Advisory?

Advisory is Matt coaching you and your team while you do the work. As Operating Partner he does the work alongside you: he’s in the building two days a week, runs the leadership cadence himself until it holds, and takes shared ownership of specific outcomes. He only does it for one company at a time.

Why is Advisory by application?

Matt works with 4 companies a year at this level so each one gets real attention. The application helps both sides see whether the timing and fit are right before anyone commits.

What happens if the cohort is full?

Request a seat anyway. You’ll be first in line for the next cohort, and Matt will tell you honestly whether it’s the right fit before you pay anything.

Can I move from one plan to another?

Yes. Plenty of owners start with the membership or a cohort and move up when the business is ready for it.

How does billing work?

Operating Partner, Advisory and the cohort are invoiced monthly, or the cohort can be paid in full up front. The membership bills monthly and you can cancel any time.

I don’t know where to start.

Take the two-minute Bottleneck Check or book a free consultation. Either one will point you in the right direction.
